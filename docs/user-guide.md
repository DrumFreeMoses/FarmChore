# FarmChore user guide

For farm workers using the app daily.

## First launch

1. The app generates your identity automatically — no sign-up needed.
2. **Set your name**: tap the badge icon → enter your first name.
3. **Scan the QR code** from your farm admin to join their farm.

## Dashboard (Today tab)

The main screen shows today's chores grouped by role. Each role card displays
the done/open count. Tap a card to see that role's full chore list.

- **Green cloud** icon = synced, all good.
- **Amber badge** = some changes waiting to sync (will push automatically).

## My Chores

The "My Chores" tab shows only the chores assigned to you. Tap any chore to:

- **Mark done** — green check, chore moves to done.
- **Skip** — yellow skip, explains why.
- **Defer** — blue arrow, moves the chore to another day.
- **Cancel** — removes it from today.

## Self-assignment

On the dashboard or morning meeting view, tap the hand icon on an unassigned
chore to assign it to yourself.

## Morning meeting

Tap the **groups icon** in the appbar to open the morning meeting summary.

Shows three sections:
- **Assigned** — chores with someone responsible.
- **Unassigned** — chores that need a volunteer.
- **Done** — already completed.

The "X remaining" counter updates in real-time. Tap any chore to self-assign
or view details.

## Messages

The **Messages** tab has two channels:

- **Farm-wide** — visible to everyone (broadcast).
- **Direct messages** — private 1:1 conversations.

Tap the pencil icon to start a new conversation. Choose "Farm-wide broadcast"
or pick a member from the list.

## Notifications

The bell icon shows notifications for:
- Chores assigned to you.
- Status changes on chores you're involved in.
- Heads-up notices from the farm.

## Heads-up notices (News tab)

Anyone can post a heads-up — weather alerts, reminders, announcements. Choose
"Whole farm" or target a specific role group.

## History

The History tab shows the last 3 months of completed, skipped, and deferred
chores — useful for tracking patterns.

## Offline use

The app works fully offline. When you reconnect:
- Your changes sync automatically.
- Other people's changes appear in real-time.
